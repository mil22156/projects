# Shelf Project — Joinery Notes

## Design

Two horizontal shelves supported by two vertical spines, joined with interlocking panel joints.

## Joint Type

**Cross-lap joint** (also called an interlocking panel joint or slot joint when used with flat panels).

Each piece gets a slot cut from its outside (front) edge:
- Slot width: ¾" (full thickness of the mating board)
- Slot depth: halfway through the board's height
- Slots go all the way through the board's thickness

The spine and shelf slide together so their faces end up flush.

## Material

Cherry (¾" stock)

## Tools

- **SUIZAN Ryoba Double Edge 9.5" (Assembly ver)** — use the crosscut edge for cheek cuts
- Chisels for waste removal and paring

## Cutting Sequence

1. Mark slot width (¾") with a marking knife on both faces
2. Mark depth line (halfway through board height) on both faces
3. Make two cheek cuts with the crosscut edge of the Ryoba down to the depth line
4. Chisel out waste between cuts, working from both faces toward the middle
5. Pare the bottom of the slot flat

## Tips

- Use a **marking knife**, not a pencil — cherry registers a knife line cleanly
- Saw on the **waste side** of the line
- Use a **shop-made saw guide** (two scraps of wood at 90°) for square cheek cuts — important with a Ryoba since it has no stiffening spine
- Sneak up on the fit — widen the slot gradually; too loose can't be fixed
- Test fit with **hand pressure only** — cherry compresses slightly under a mallet
